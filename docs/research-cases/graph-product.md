Let G and H be finite simple undirected graphs, and let \(\chi\) denote chromatic number. Define their categorical product \(G\times H\) to have vertex set \(V(G)\times V(H)\), with \((g,h)\) adjacent to \((g',h')\) exactly when \(gg'\in E(G)\) and \(hh'\in E(H)\).

Determine whether
\[
\chi(G\times H)=\min\{\chi(G),\chi(H)\}
\]
holds for every such pair \(G,H\).
