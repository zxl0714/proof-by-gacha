For each of the following two inequalities, determine all real numbers \(r>0\) for which it holds for every positive integer \(n\) and every pair of Hermitian positive semidefinite matrices \(A,B\in M_n(\mathbb C)\):
\[
\operatorname{tr}(A+B)^r\leq \operatorname{tr}(A^r)+\operatorname{tr}(B^r),
\]
\[
(A+B)^r\preceq A^r+B^r.
\]
Here matrix powers are defined by spectral functional calculus, with \(0^r=0\), and \(X\preceq Y\) means that \(Y-X\) is positive semidefinite.
